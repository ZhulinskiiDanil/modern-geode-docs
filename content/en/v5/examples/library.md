## Complete examples, one concept at a time

Start with a small change you can verify. Each example below links to complete source in an article, prerequisites, expected behavior, and common mistakes. A short code fragment alone is not a complete mod.

## Choose your next project

Use difficulty, topic and platform filters. The platform labels describe source targets, not a claim that these examples were compiled and tested on your hardware. Build and test before distribution.

## A reliable practice loop

Read the explanation, make one change, build, install, then verify in the game. Keep a working copy before experimenting. If a build fails, reduce the change until you can explain the failure.
